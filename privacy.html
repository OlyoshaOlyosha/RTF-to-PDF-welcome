Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains the information collection, use, and sharing practices of RTF to PDF ("we," "us," and "our").

Unless otherwise stated, this Policy describes and governs the information collection, use, and sharing practices of RTF to PDF with respect to your use of our Chrome Extension (the "Services").

Before you use the Services, please carefully review this Privacy Policy. By using any part of the Services, you understand that your information will be handled as outlined in this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use our Services.

Our Principles

RTF to PDF has designed this policy to be consistent with the following principles:

  • Local Processing First: Data processing should be simplified as much as possible to enhance security. Your files should never leave your device.
  • Minimal Data Footprint: We believe that the safest data is the data that is never collected.
  • Transparency: Privacy practices should be human-readable, easy to find, and meet the reasonable expectations of users.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly to Us

We do not require you to create an online account, make donations, or provide any personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or payment information) to use our Services.

Information that Is Automatically Collected

We do not automatically collect any device information, IP addresses, location data, browser types, or usage statistics.

Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies

We do not use cookies, web server logs, web beacons, tracking pixels, or any other tracking technologies within the Extension.

Information from Third Parties

We do not collect, purchase, or receive any information about you from third parties.

How We Use Your Information

Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not use, share, or sell your data. All file conversions (RTF to PDF) are processed locally within your browser's memory. Your files are never uploaded to, stored on, or transmitted to our servers.

Permissions and Local Storage

To function properly, the Extension requires the following browser permissions:

  • downloads: Used solely to save the converted PDF files to your computer.
  • storage & unlimitedStorage: Used to temporarily store your file queue in your browser's local database (IndexedDB) to prevent data loss if you close the Extension popup. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Security Measures

Because all processing happens locally on your device, your files never leave your computer. We do not store or transmit your files. While no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, processing data locally ensures that your files are not exposed to external network risks.

Children

The Services are intended for users over the age of 18 and are not directed to children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from children under the age of 13, we will take reasonable steps to delete it as soon as practicable. (Note: We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone).

Data Retention

We do not retain any personal information. Any temporary data (such as your file queue) is stored locally on your device and can be cleared by you at any time.
